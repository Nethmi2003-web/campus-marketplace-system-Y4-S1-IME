# CampusCart data notes

The public CampusCart pages preserve the figures from the student business plan. Where the source sections disagree, this site keeps the most relevant figure for the page and does not silently reconcile the source.

1. **Platform fee and vendor commission:** the plan describes a 5–8% platform fee in customer-facing sections and an 8–12% vendor commission in vendor/pricing sections. The site uses 5–8% in the public value proposition and 8–12% for vendor commission.
2. **Brand name:** the source uses both “UniSwap – Campus Cart” and “CampusCart.” The company/about information uses “UniSwap – Campus Cart”; product UI uses “CampusCart.”
3. **Assets and initial investment:** the production plan lists Rs 45,000 for asset costs while the financial/depreciation plan lists Rs 1,200,000. Initial investment is also stated as Rs 2,355,000 in assumptions and Rs 1,245,000 as cost of project. Funding and asset pages show the financial-plan cost-of-project and depreciation figures; the differing production-plan figure is retained here for review.
4. **Margins:** financial ratios state gross-profit ratios of 99.77%, 99.83% and 99.84%, and net-profit ratios of 67.81%, 68.64% and 69.15%. Other sections describe 28% Q1 gross margin, a 25–30% target and a 35%+ objective. These statements are shown in their relevant contexts; they are not reconciled.
5. **Currency:** some reward assumptions use USD ($3–7 per delivery and $5 referral), while the rest of the plan uses LKR. Customer-facing pricing uses the LKR figures supplied for referrals and does not convert the USD assumptions.
6. **Labour and annexes:** labour totals differ between Annexure VI, the production-cost table and cash flow, and two annexes are both numbered “Annexure V.” The labour roles are listed without inventing a reconciled total.
7. **Cash flow:** monthly cash-flow values are labelled as LKR thousands, while loan-funds entries mix full-LKR figures and the Y1–Y3 loan-funds row is inconsistent. The monthly figures are displayed as supplied and the unit caveat is shown beside the chart.
8. **Loan interest:** the plan-stated yearly interest totals (Rs 142,333.33 / Rs 86,333.33 / Rs 30,333.33) are preserved separately from the interactive calculator. The calculator recomputes a reducing-balance schedule from the entered 14% annual rate, principal and term; it is not presented as a reconciliation of those plan totals.
9. **Marketing costs:** the detailed cost build-up totals Rs 17,030,500 / Rs 28,735,000 / Rs 43,000,000, while the projected P&L separately lists marketing & distribution of Rs 192,000 / Rs 201,000 / Rs 161,000. Both sets are reproduced in context.
10. **Competitor analysis:** the brief provides competitor categories and general strengths/limitations, but not a full named-company advertising dataset. The market table uses those categories and does not invent competitor-specific advertising spend.
11. **Calculator assumptions:** the pricing calculator uses the 5% lower end of the CampusCart platform-fee range, the lower stated delivery fee (Rs 492) and the lower stated third-party app fee endpoint (30% of basket value) for an illustrative comparison. It excludes the first-five-order launch offer from recurring monthly estimates. The calculation is a fee comparison, not a complete basket or seller-settlement model, and does not guarantee savings.
12. **Customer quotes and visuals:** sample testimonials are explicitly marked as placeholders. The app preview is a static illustrative mockup, not a live vendor feed or map.
13. **Monthly cash-flow sales total:** the twelve monthly sales values add to 18,800 (LKR thousands), while the plan states a total of 19,800. Both the monthly series and plan-reported total are displayed as supplied; the difference is not reconciled.
